## 0.1.0 — first release

For Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1, Create 6.0.10.

### Machines
- Brewing kettle: water and ingredients over a fire become wort. Any mix brews; the closest recipe decides the drink, and inaccuracy costs stars.
- Distillery: a two-block still driven by a Create shaft at the back; speed decides quality.
- Aging barrel: barrels placed side by side join into one bigger barrel; the right wood and the right number of days make a perfect drink.
- Spout and Create integration: pipes, pumps, funnels, tanks and Create's own spout move wort and fill bottles, keeping quality.

### Drinks
- Eleven drink chains, from beer and cider to tequila, absinthe and firefly liqueur. Recipes are hidden: players discover them by experimenting.
- Quality from 1 to 5 stars on every drink; mixes that match nothing become the muddy brew.
- Each drink has its own colour and its own effects, stronger the better the drink.

### Drunkenness
- Camera sway, slurred chat and passing out when overdone.
- Staggering while walking, throwing up when very drunk, and a hangover after bad drinks.

### Other
